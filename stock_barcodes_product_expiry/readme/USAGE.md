Confirm or not receipt of expired product
------------------------
1. When validating a receipt from the barcode screen and one of the products is expired, a confirmation wizard will appear to allow you to accept or reject that product, or to validate only valid receipts.
2. If you confirm the receipt, validation will proceed.
